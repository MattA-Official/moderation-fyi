The moderation.fyi team is currently one volunteer moderator who runs this site ([Matt Adcock](/authors/matt-adcock)). They moderate across Discord, Reddit, and Twitch, and started moderation.fyi because they picked up on the barriers facing new moderators.

Pages credited to the team will be ones we've written together, or ones that have been reworked by enough people that no single name fits. They will follow the same principles as everything else here: each one should help you do something today, work on most platforms, and treat members with respect, including the ones being moderated.

We also look after the site itself: reviewing contributions, keeping guides up to date, and fixing things when they break. Everything is published openly on GitHub, and anyone can join in, whether that's writing a guide, sharing a template or fixing a typo. [contact@moderation.fyi](mailto:contact@moderation.fyi).
