Sam Okafor has moderated online communities since 2016, starting on a 300-member Minecraft server and working up to head of safety for a 60,000-member Discord server built around a competitive strategy game. They also help run the game's subreddit, mostly on the modmail side.

Sam's mod team is known for keeping notes on everything. They track raids, appeals and rule changes in a shared spreadsheet, which is how the team noticed that most of its raid traffic arrived in the first ten minutes after an account joined. That spreadsheet became the basis for Sam's first article here.

Sam is most interested in the unglamorous parts of moderation: onboarding new mods, writing ban messages people actually read, and measuring whether a change made things better or just felt like it did. They write for moderation.fyi when they've tried something long enough to have numbers to share.

Outside moderation, Sam works in IT support and plays far less of the game they moderate than anyone assumes.
