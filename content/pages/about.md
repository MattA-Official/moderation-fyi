## Why we exist

Millions of online communities, including Discord servers, subreddits, Twitch channels, forums and group chats, are kept running by volunteers. Most moderators learn the job on the fly, often in the middle of a crisis, with little more than advice scattered across old forum threads and platform help centres.

**moderation.fyi** collects that hard-won knowledge in one place, so that every moderator can benefit from what others have already learnt.

## Our principles

- **Practical over theoretical.** Every resource should help you do something today.
- **Platform-agnostic.** Good moderation principles apply everywhere. Where platform specifics matter, we say so.
- **Kind and fair.** We favour approaches that treat community members with respect, including the ones being moderated.
- **Free and open.** No paywalls and no accounts. Everything is published openly on GitHub.

## Who's behind it

moderation.fyi is an open-source project maintained by volunteer moderators. Anyone can [contribute](/contribute), whether that means writing a guide, sharing a template, fixing a typo or improving the website itself.
