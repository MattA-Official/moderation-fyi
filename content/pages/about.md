## Why we exist

Millions of online communities, including Discord servers, subreddits, Twitch channels, forums and group chats, are kept running by volunteers. Most moderators learn the job on the fly, often in the middle of a crisis, with little more than advice scattered across old forum threads and platform help centres.

moderation.fyi collects that hard-won knowledge in one place, so every mod can use what others have already learnt.

## Our principles

- Every page should help you do something today. Theory comes after, if at all.
- Good moderation works the same way on most platforms. Where a platform's settings matter, we say so.
- We favour approaches that treat members with respect, including the ones being moderated.
- No paywalls and no accounts. Everything is published openly on GitHub, and contributors are credited.

## Who's behind it

moderation.fyi is an open-source project maintained by volunteer moderators. You can [meet the authors](/authors), and anyone can [contribute](/contribute), whether that means writing a guide, sharing a template, fixing a typo or improving the website itself.
