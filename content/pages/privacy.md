We try to collect as little information about you as possible. This page explains what we do collect and why.

## Browsing the site

You don't need an account to read anything on moderation.fyi. We don't use advertising or third-party analytics, and we don't set tracking cookies.

The site is hosted on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/), which processes technical information such as your IP address and browser details in order to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. These logs are kept for a short period and are used only to keep the site running.

Your browser's local storage is used to remember a small number of preferences, such as light or dark mode and whether you've dismissed the announcement banner. This information never leaves your device.

## Newsletter

If you subscribe to updates, we store:

- your email address
- your name, if you choose to give it
- the date you subscribed

We use this information only to email you about new content on moderation.fyi. We will never sell or share it with anyone else for marketing. It is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database.

You can ask us to remove you from the list at any time by [opening an issue](https://github.com/MattA-Official/moderation-fyi/issues/new) or replying to any email we send.

## Contributing

If you contribute through GitHub, your GitHub username and any information you choose to include will be public, in line with [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, [get in touch via GitHub](https://github.com/MattA-Official/moderation-fyi/issues/new).

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the bottom of this page.
